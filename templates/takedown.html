DMCA Takedown Process

To submit a valid DMCA notice, please provide the following information as required by 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3):

  1. Your name and contact information (email required for us to respond).
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed.
  3. The Pinterest URL of the infringing content.
  4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  5. A statement that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorised to act on their behalf.
