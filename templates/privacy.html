Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2024

⚠️ PinFetch is not affiliated with Pinterest, Inc. This tool helps you download content from publicly accessible Pinterest URLs. Download only content you own or have permission to use.

1. Information We Collect

PinFetch is designed to be privacy-first. We do not store, log, or share any of the following:

  • Pinterest URLs you enter into the downloader
  • Files you download via PinFetch
  • Your name, email address, or account information (unless you submit a takedown request)
  • Browsing history or usage patterns beyond anonymous server logs

Your download preferences (e.g. preferred quality) may be stored locally in your browser's localStorage — this data never leaves your device.

2. Server Logs

Like all web servers, PinFetch records standard HTTP request logs (IP address, request path, timestamp, response status). These logs are used solely for debugging, security monitoring, and abuse prevention. They are not sold or shared with third parties and are retained for a maximum of 30 days.

3. Cookies

PinFetch does not use tracking cookies or session cookies. If Google AdSense is displayed on this site, Google may set cookies to serve relevant advertisements (see Section 5 below).

4. Takedown Requests

If you submit a takedown request via our Takedown Request page, we collect:

  • Your name and email address (to respond to your request)
  • The Pinterest URL you believe infringes your rights
  • A description of the alleged infringement

This information is stored securely and used only to process your request. Takedown log entries are retained for a maximum of 90 days, after which they are deleted.

5. Google AdSense

This site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

Google AdSense is governed by Google's own Privacy Policy and AdSense programme policies.

6. Third-Party Services

PinFetch uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — to load the Inter typeface. Font requests are sent to Google's servers; see Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Pinterest CDN (pinimg.com) — media files are streamed directly from Pinterest's content delivery network. Pinterest's Privacy Policy applies to that traffic.

7. Pinterest API Usage

PinFetch fetches publicly available Pinterest content in accordance with Pinterest's Developer Guidelines and Terms of Service. We do not circumvent any authentication or access control mechanism.

8. Children's Privacy

PinFetch is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of PinFetch after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
