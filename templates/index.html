Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about downloading Pinterest videos, reels, and photos with PinFetch.

How do I download a Pinterest video using PinFetch? ▼

Downloading is simple and takes three quick steps:

  1. Open Pinterest, find the video or reel you want, tap the Share icon, and select Copy Link.
  2. Open PinFetch in any browser, paste the copied link into the search bar, and tap Download.
  3. Choose your desired resolution (1080p Full HD, 720p HD, etc.) and tap Download .MP4 to save it directly to your device.
How to use the Pinterest downloader on Android? ▼

In the Pinterest Android app, tap the three dots or Share arrow on any pin and select Copy Link. Then open Chrome, visit PinFetch, tap the Paste button, and tap Download. The video or image will download straight into your device's Downloads folder and immediately appear in your Google Photos or Gallery app. You can also tap Chrome menu → "Install app" to install PinFetch as a home screen PWA.

Can I download Pinterest videos on iPhone and iPad? ▼

Yes! PinFetch is fully optimized for iOS and iPadOS Safari:

  1. Copy the pin link from the Pinterest app.
  2. Paste into PinFetch in Safari and tap Download.
  3. Tap Download Best Quality MP4. When Safari asks, tap Download.
  4. Tap the blue download circle in Safari's address bar, select the downloaded file, tap the Share button, and choose "Save Video" to move it into your Photos Camera Roll.
How to convert Pinterest video to MP4? ▼

PinFetch converts videos to MP4 automatically! Many Pinterest reels and story videos are stored as fragmented HLS (.m3u8) streams. Our backend server dynamically remuxes these streams into universal, standalone .MP4 files on-the-fly using ffmpeg. You don't need any third-party conversion software — every video downloaded from PinFetch is guaranteed to be a native MP4 that plays on any media player.

Can I download private Pinterest videos or secret boards? ▼

No. PinFetch respects user privacy and copyright guidelines. We can only extract and download content from public pins that can be viewed without logging into a private account. Content inside secret boards, private group boards, or password-protected pins cannot be retrieved.

Does PinFetch track a user's downloaded videos or browsing activity? ▼

No. PinFetch operates on a strict zero-tracking policy. We do not store copies of your downloaded videos or photos on our servers, and we never log your IP address or browsing habits. Your "Recent Downloads" list is stored purely on your own device inside your browser's private local storage (localStorage), and you can clear it anytime with a single click.

Where is the video or image saved after download? ▼

The save location depends on your operating system:

  • Android: Saved directly to your device's /Download folder and visible in your Gallery or Photos app.
  • iPhone / iPad: Saved to Safari's Downloads (in the Files app). Tap the download arrow → Share → "Save Video" to add it to your Photos Camera Roll.
  • PC & Mac: Saved to your operating system's standard Downloads folder (e.g. C:\Users\YourName\Downloads or /Users/YourName/Downloads).
Do I need to register an account to download videos? ▼

No account or registration is required. You never have to sign up, provide an email address, or enter personal information to use PinFetch. Just open the website, paste a link, and download your media instantly.

Does PinFetch add watermarks to downloaded videos or photos? ▼

No, PinFetch never adds watermarks, logos, or overlays to any video or photo. You get the pure, untouched original file exactly as published by the Pinterest creator.

Is PinFetch safe for my phone, tablet, and PC? ▼

Yes, PinFetch is 100% safe, clean, and secure. PinFetch is a web-based service that streams media directly through encrypted HTTPS connections. There is no software, extension, or executable to install, meaning zero risk of viruses, spyware, malware, or suspicious redirect popups.

Is PinFetch completely free to use? ▼

Yes, PinFetch is 100% free with unlimited downloads. There are no daily limits, subscriptions, speed throttling, or hidden paywalls. You can download as many videos, reels, photos, and music tracks as you wish.

Does PinFetch support short links like pin.it and multi-photo carousel pins? ▼

Yes! PinFetch automatically follows and resolves short URLs (like https://pin.it/...) generated by the mobile Pinterest app. Additionally, if you paste a link to a carousel pin with multiple photos, PinFetch detects all images and provides an interactive gallery with 1-click download buttons for each individual high-resolution picture.
